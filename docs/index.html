Gofer

Tell it what you need. It finds it, you approve, it pays.

An iPhone shopping agent that pays with PayPal. Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon 2026.

Demo build: Gofer runs against the PayPal sandbox. No real money moves and no real orders are placed with retailers. Checkout needs a PayPal sandbox account.
