Gofer

Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Gofer is a shopping agent for iPhone. This policy explains what the app sends to our server and to the services it uses. Gofer has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal. You sign in and approve payments on PayPal's own pages; Gofer does not receive your PayPal password or payment card details. This version uses the PayPal sandbox, so no real money is charged. PayPal's handling of your data is covered by the PayPal privacy statement.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database.
  • DeepSeek processes the text of your requests to decide which products to search for and how to describe them.
  • Channel3 searches retailer catalogues and returns product details and prices.
  • PayPal processes payments and stores your saved wallet.
  • Apple delivers push notifications.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Order, rule, watch and device records are kept until you ask us to delete them or the demo service is shut down. Settings → Data → "Reset this device" makes the app forget your ID and start fresh; deleting the app does the same. To delete the server-side records, open an issue with the device ID shown in Settings and we will remove them within 30 days. You can turn off automatic purchases at any time by setting the limit to "Always ask".

Children

Gofer is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
