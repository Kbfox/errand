Support

Questions, bugs or data requests: open an issue on GitHub. We usually answer within two working days.

Is any real money charged?

No. This version of Gofer is a demo connected to the PayPal sandbox. Payments are simulated by PayPal, nothing is charged, and no order is placed with a retailer.

How do I pay in the app?

When PayPal opens, sign in with a PayPal sandbox personal account. A regular PayPal account will not work in the sandbox. You can create sandbox accounts for free at developer.paypal.com.

How do the spending rules work?

Your first purchase is always approved by you in PayPal. If you allow it, PayPal saves your wallet for Gofer. In the Rules tab you can then set "Buy without asking under" and a monthly cap. Purchases inside those limits are charged to the saved sandbox wallet without opening PayPal; anything else opens PayPal for your approval. Set the limit to "Always ask" to turn automatic purchases off.

How do price watches work?

Ask Gofer to buy a product if it drops under a price. Gofer re-checks the price every 15 minutes and buys only within your rules. Stop a watch in the Orders tab.

How do I delete my data?

Gofer has no accounts. Settings → Data → "Reset this device" (or deleting the app) makes Gofer forget the random ID that links your data on our server. To have the server-side records removed as well, open an issue with the device ID shown in Settings and we will delete them. See the privacy policy.
